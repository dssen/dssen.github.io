05 / STORY

FROM CODE
TO CAMPAIGNS.

EARLY

THE BEGINNING

I was the kid who took the computer apart to see how it worked. That never really stopped — it just got more expensive.

2017

BACHELOR'S DEGREE

Graduated with an IT degree and a conviction that software was where everything interesting was going to happen. I was right — just not about which part.

2017—18

IT MANAGER · ARALIYA GREEN HILLS HOTEL

My first job put me inside a hotel. I learned the business from the inside — occupancy, OTA commissions, what actually drives a booking, what a slow season does to a P&L. Most marketers in hospitality learn this from the outside. I got it first.

2018—24

SOFTWARE ENGINEER · SIX YEARS

Six years of building, shipping, debugging and maintaining real systems. It taught me the thing that matters most in marketing: if you can't measure it, you're not managing it — you're hoping.

PIVOT

LEARNING DIGITAL MARKETING

Somewhere in those six years I started running campaigns at night. Ads, analytics, funnels, SEO. It felt like the same work — systems, inputs, outputs, iteration — just with a market on the other end instead of a compiler.

FREE—LANCE

FIRST CLIENTS

Hotels. Restaurants. Travel agencies. Vehicle rentals. Businesses with genuinely great products and marketing that wasn't carrying them. I fixed that on evenings and weekends — until the evenings and weekends weren't enough.

NOW

FULL-TIME DIGITAL MARKETING SPECIALIST

I went full-time. Today I run social, Google Ads, OTA channels and websites for hospitality and travel brands across Sri Lanka — with an engineer's instinct for measurement behind every decision.

NEXT

GEO & AI VISIBILITY

Search is being rewritten. I'm deep in GEO — making sure the brands I work with are the ones AI recommends, not the ones it's never heard of. Most businesses here haven't started. That's exactly why now is the moment.
