07 / FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the ad budget included in social media packages?

No. Ad budget is paid directly by you to Meta or TikTok, so you always see exactly where your money goes. We manage the campaigns.

Is there a contract?

Social media packages have a 3-month minimum, then continue month to month. Design work is a one-time payment.

How do payments work?

Social media packages are billed monthly in advance. Design projects need a 50% advance, with the balance paid on final approval before files are delivered.

Do I own the designs?

Yes. You get full ownership rights for every logo and design once the project is paid in full.

Do you create content in Sinhala and Tamil?

Yes. Captions and designs can be in Sinhala, English or Tamil depending on your package and audience.

Do I need to provide photos?

We work with your product photos and videos. If you don’t have any, you can book a half-day shoot as an add-on (included in Premium).

Do unused posts roll over?

No. Each month’s posts are planned and published within that month.

Can I get a custom package?

Absolutely. Message us with what you need and we’ll put together a quote.

Prices are in LKR and exclude any applicable taxes. Prices may change; the quote confirmed at the start of your project applies.
